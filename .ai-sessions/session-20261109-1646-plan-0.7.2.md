**What could improve:**
- I told Mason the plan had 11 steps; it has 12. Count unchecked items from todo.md instead of recalling the section map.
- plan.md and todo.md were left untracked after `/bpe:plan`, which guarantees a goal pre-flight refusal. Commit them as a planning commit before suggesting `/bpe:goal`.

**Course corrections:**
- A stale `goal.md` from the 0.5.0 era (branch `fable`) was still in the working tree. It is gitignored and gets overwritten by this run.
